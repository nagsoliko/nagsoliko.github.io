corn & potato chowder

4 ears corn cut corn off cobs and reserve separately.
250g bacon, chopped cook bacon. remove and reserve, leaving fat in pot.
30g (2tbsp) butter
holy trinity		 add butter and holy trinity. cook till softened.
1-2 garlic cl., minced add garlic.
60g flour whisk flour into fat. make white roux.
reserved cobs
600g potatoes
1 bay leaf
oregano
pepper
750ml milk
water & stock		 add ingredients, cover and simmer for ~25mins.
uncover and remove cobs.
reserved corn
extra corn		 add corn and cook to liking.
185ml cream
reserved bacon
scallions		 mix in cream, bacon and scallions.

bacon: can be substituted with any cured, fatty pork (like prosciutto), or left out.
holy trinity: mixture of chopped onions, celery, and bell peppers, being roughly 2:1:1 by volume. carrots can substitute for bell peppers (french mirepoix, italian battuto/soffrito), or for celery.
white roux: flour fried, with fat and flour being roughly 1:1 by volume. fried enough to cook off the rawness of flour, but not enough to brown. best made with a whisk.
cream: can be substituted by more milk and butter, to taste.
toppings: like fried bacon, scallions, chilli oil, etc.
