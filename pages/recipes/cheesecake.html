baked cheesecake

crumb crust

200g plain biscuits
100g powd. sugar		 blitz together in food processor.
120g butter melt and mix into crushed biscuits till it resembles wet sand.
press mixture into greased springform cake mould.

plain biscuits: any plain crackers like graham crackers or digestive biscuits will work.

sugar: either brown or white, or a mix of the two, will work. alternatively, white sugar with molasses can be used instead.

butter: either salted or unsalted will work. optionally, add a pinch of salt if using unsalted.

cheese batter

500g cream cheese beat till smooth.
2tbsp all-purpose flour beat in till just combined.
300g powd. white sugar
1tsp vanilla extract
125g sour cream
zest of 1 lemon		 beat in ingredients till just combined.
3 large eggs beat in one by one till just combined and batter is smooth.

sour cream: can be substituted with yoghurt. should be omitted if using spreadable cream cheese instead of block cream cheese.

cheesecake

crust
batter		 pour batter into crust prepared in mould.
bake in preheated oven at 160°C for ~55mins till lightly golden-brown on top and slightly jiggly in the centre.
cool and refrigerate for ≥4hrs before serving.

toppings: like berries, jams, icing sugar, etc.
